2. Description of Service

AttenDO is a free attendance tracking application designed for students. The Service allows you to:

  • Track attendance across multiple subjects
  • Manage your class timetable and schedule
  • View attendance statistics and analytics
  • Calculate safe bunk allowances based on attendance targets
  • Export attendance data in CSV, PDF, or JSON format
  • Sync data across devices using cloud storage
  • Use an AI-powered chatbot (powered by Google Gemini) that answers natural language questions about your attendance, predicts safe bunks, and provides weekly summaries
  • Upload a photo of your timetable for AI-powered automatic schedule extraction (powered by Google Gemini Vision)
  • Receive browser push notifications for class reminders and attendance alerts

The Service is available as a web application at attendotracker.netlify.app and as an Android app (com.subha.attendo).
